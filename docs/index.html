Three keys, the whole computer.

Ghost Cursor reads the real buttons in your apps and asks Claude for the single next step. It never clicks without your keypress.

⌥G

Say a goal

“Export this frame as PNG.” “Turn on dark mode.” Plain words, any app.

⌥Space

Accept the step

The ghost glides to the next click. It's solid when sure and faint when guessing. Accept and it clicks for you.

⌥Esc

Or correct it

Click the right thing yourself. Ghost Cursor records your correction and carries on from there.

Every keypress is an eval.

Accepts, rejects and corrections become a live benchmark of where computer-use models work in real apps and where they break.

Agreement by app. See which apps the model handles and which it doesn't.
Failure modes by element. Icon-only buttons, canvases, custom dropdowns.
Calibration. Check whether high confidence actually means right.
Stays on your Mac. The log is a local JSONL file. Nothing is uploaded.
